# Azan: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 22 October 2026_

Azan ("the app") is a Samsung Smart TV app that shows prayer times, plays the Azan, plays Quran recitations and shows the local weather, in Arabic and English. This policy explains what information the app uses.

## Information we collect
**We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer runs no server that receives your data.

## Information stored on your TV
The app saves the following only on your TV, in the app's local storage:
- your settings, including the city you chose for prayer times and the app language
- your last Quran playback position
- the approximate location detected from your IP address (city, country and coordinates), the last weather reading, and the Quran reciter list, so the app works offline

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## Third-party services the app contacts
To provide its features, the app makes requests directly from your TV to these services. As with any internet request, they receive your TV's public IP address.

| Service | Purpose | Data sent |
|---|---|---|
| BigDataCloud (api.bigdatacloud.net) | Find your approximate city from your IP address, for automatic prayer times and the weather. This is not used if you choose a city in Settings | Your IP address |
| ipwho.is | Backup for the same | Your IP address |
| Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) | Current weather | The latitude/longitude of your detected or chosen city |
| mp3quran.net | Quran recitations, ayah timings, Mushaf page images and the reciter list | The surah and reciter you chose |
| picsum.photos / Unsplash | Daily landscape background photo | None, beyond the standard request |

Each service handles requests under its own privacy policy. The app sends no other information to them.

Prayer times, the sun and moon positions, and the Azan audio are calculated or stored on the TV and need no internet connection.

## Children
The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
